The short answer

To work out how many days until your holiday, count the calendar days from today to the first day of the trip: a holiday on Saturday is one day away on Friday, whatever the time. The countdown above does exactly that, then shows the weeks, hours and minutes as well.

How the countdown counts

It counts calendar days, not 24-hour blocks. If you fly on 14 July and today is 13 July, that's one day to go, whether it's 7am or 11pm. That's how people actually think about a holiday, and it's how the countdown in the Travary app works: the number only changes at midnight, and a clock change or a time zone can't nudge it.

The smaller numbers under the postcard are the live part: the weeks, then the hours, minutes and seconds left until midnight at the start of your first day, in your own time zone.

Nothing you type is sent anywhere. The countdown runs in your browser, and the share link simply carries the trip name, place and dates in the address, so whoever opens it sees the same postcard.

Ways to make the wait part of the holiday

The weeks before a trip are some of the best bits of it, especially with children. A few that work:

  • A paper chain. One link per day, torn off each morning. Cheap, satisfying, and it makes "how many sleeps?" answer itself.
  • Milestone days. Pick a few dates to look forward to on the way: 30 days (book the airport parking), 14 days (check the passports, order currency), 7 days (start the packing pile), 1 day (check in online).
  • A taste of the place. Cook something from where you're going on the 10-day mark, or watch a film set there. It gets children asking questions you'd want them asking anyway.
  • Let everyone see it. A countdown on the family group chat, or the fridge, keeps everyone pointed at the same date, which makes the logistics easier too.

For the to-do side of those milestones, our checklist of things to do before going on holiday sorts them by how far ahead you need to do them.

Counting down to Disney World, a cruise or a first flight

The bigger the trip, the longer the countdown tends to run. A theme park holiday is often booked a year out, so the number starts in the hundreds; a cruise has a fixed sailing date, so the first day is easy. For a first flight with small children, a short countdown (two weeks or so) usually works better than a long one, because a hundred days means nothing to a four-year-old.

If you're going to Orlando, the Orlando itinerary with kids lays out a week at a pace children can manage, and it's a good thing to read on day 30.

A countdown on your phone

You can bookmark this page with your link and add it to your home screen: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; in Chrome on Android, open the menu and choose Add to Home screen. It opens straight onto your postcard.

Your phone's calendar can count down too: add the trip as an all-day event and most calendar widgets show the days until it. It's plain, but it works.
