No folders to file things in and no trip to set up before you start. Add what you've booked; Travary works out the rest.

Today

Built for the morning of, not just the planning.

Most travel apps are built for researching a trip. Travary is built for being on one. During a holiday it opens on today; before it, on the first day of the next trip. The day's cards run down a time rail: the next ticket in its place, your stay's key card after it, then the tables and tours, with finished things folding away under "Earlier".

  • Tickets one tap away, offline
  • Times exactly as printed on the ticket, in the local time where they happen
  • Swipe for tomorrow, or tap the date for the whole trip

Between trips

When you're home, it counts down.

Between holidays, Today becomes a postcard from wherever you're going next, with a luggage tag counting the days. It slaps on fresh every time you open the app, which is a small thing that makes the wait feel shorter.

Inside a trip

Every trip on one page, with the gaps spotted for you.

Open a trip and your plan reads like a ledger: each booking on its date, its ticket on the row. Travary notices what's missing, like nights without a stay or the flight home, and the trip's "Before you go" list keeps the to-dos that aren't bookings, like the visa, the pet sitter and the travel money, in the same place.

  • Nights without a stay and the missing flight home, flagged
  • A shared "Before you go" checklist with "by" dates
  • Dates kept honest: bookings stay inside their trip

Travel documents

Your passport stays on your phone. Only your phone.

Passports, visas and insurance live in Travel documents: stored only on this phone, behind its own Face ID, fingerprint or passcode, and locked again the moment you leave the app. They're never uploaded, never shared with the family and never read by AI. Travary checks each one against your next trip, so a passport that runs out too soon is spotted while there's still time to renew.

  • Numbers stay masked until you ask
  • Expiry checked against the next trip's dates
  • Every ticket and booking in the Documents tab, offline
