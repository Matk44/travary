Choose TripIt if…

  • You fly often for work and want flight delay and gate alerts.
  • Your bookings all arrive by email and you're happy to forward them.
  • You track loyalty points and want them in the same app.

Choose Travary if…

  • It's a holiday, and you want each day laid out, not just a list of reservations.
  • You're organising for a family or group, on a mix of iPhones and Android phones.
  • You'd rather not give an app your email: add a booking from a screenshot or PDF instead.
  • You want the countdown, the trip's gaps spotted, and a checklist for the jobs that aren't bookings.
  • You want passports kept privately on your phone, not uploaded anywhere.

Can you use both?

Yes, and some people will. A frequent flyer might keep TripIt for its flight alerts on work trips and use Travary for family holidays, where the day-by-day plan and sharing with the family matter more than the flights. For the full field, see TripIt alternatives and the best itinerary apps.
