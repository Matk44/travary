Choose Wanderlog if…

  • You haven't decided what to do yet and want suggestions and a map.
  • You're planning a road trip or a multi-city route and want to see it drawn out.
  • Your group wants to edit the plan together, for free, before you book.

Choose Travary if…

  • You've booked, and want it all in one place, day by day, with the tickets.
  • You need it to work offline without paying for it.
  • It's a family holiday, and the morning-of view matters more than the map.
  • You want passports kept privately and checked against the trip.

Plan in one, travel with the other

The two don't really compete. Wanderlog is at its best in the weeks of research before a trip; Travary is at its best from the first booking to the last day. If you plan with a map and friends, do that, then add what you actually book to Travary, which turns it into each morning's plan, offline. For other options, see the best itinerary apps, compared.
