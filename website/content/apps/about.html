What it does

  • Bookings first: add a flight, stay, ticket, table, hire car or note; only a title and date are required. Trips form themselves from the dates.
  • Smart Import: pick a screenshot, photo or PDF of a confirmation and it's read into bookings to check.
  • Today: the day's plan on a time rail, the next ticket ready, finished things folded away.
  • Countdown: between trips, a postcard from the next destination with a luggage tag counting the days.
  • Inside a trip: the plan as a ledger by date, gaps spotted (nights without a stay, the flight home), and a shared "Before you go" checklist.
  • Documents: every ticket in one place, offline; passports, visas and insurance on the phone only, behind Face ID, with expiry checked against the trip.
  • Destination looks: postcards and ticket art drawn for 30 places, chosen from the trip's name.
  • Local time: every time kept exactly as printed on the ticket.

What it doesn't do

Travary doesn't sell travel, suggest places to visit, show maps or read your email. It organises what you've booked, and on a trip with a pass it tells you when a flight changes.
