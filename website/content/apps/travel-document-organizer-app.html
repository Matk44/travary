Two kinds of document, two kinds of care

"Travel documents" means two quite different things. Booking documents (boarding passes, hotel confirmations, tickets, car hire vouchers) need to be quick to find, offline, and often shared with the people you're travelling with. Identity documents (passports, visas, insurance policies) need the opposite: to be private, locked, and never shared by accident.

Most apps treat them the same way, which usually means putting a passport scan somewhere it shouldn't be. Travary keeps them apart.

Tickets and bookings: the Documents tab

Every booking for every trip, with its ticket, gathered in one tab as a row of postage stamps, searchable and filtered by kind. Tickets are stored on your phone with their bookings, so they open in aeroplane mode. On a shared trip, everyone's tickets are backed up and downloaded to each phone.
