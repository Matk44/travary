Planning the holiday you've booked

Search for a "holiday app" and you'll mostly find booking sites and tour operators' apps. They're good at selling you a holiday and at showing the parts you bought from them. What they don't do is hold the whole thing: the flights from one company, the villa from another, the airport parking, the boat trip, the restaurant you booked yourself.

Travary is for that part. It doesn't sell anything; it organises what you've already booked, wherever you booked it.

From confirmation email to day-by-day plan

  1. Add your bookings

    Type them in (only a title and a date are needed), or pick the confirmation PDF or a screenshot and Smart Import reads it. A package holiday's single confirmation, with flights, hotel and transfer, becomes several bookings for you to check.

  2. The holiday forms itself

    Bookings group into a trip by their dates. Call it "Majorca with the kids" and Travary knows where you're going, and dresses the trip with that place's postcard.

  3. Each day, in order

    On holiday, the app opens on today: the transfer time, the hotel's key card, the tables you've booked. Tickets are one tap away, even with no signal.

The weeks before you go

Between holidays, Travary counts down on a postcard from your destination, with a luggage tag showing the days to go. Each trip also has a "Before you go" list for the jobs that aren't bookings: the GHIC card, the travel money, the cattery, the airport parking. Give a job a "by" date and share the list with whoever's doing half of it. Our holiday checklist has the full list, sorted by when to do each job.

Travary also checks every passport you add against the holiday. Europe's rule (three months left after you leave, and issued within the last ten years) catches people out; Travary flags any passport with less than six months left after you're home, which covers every country's rule. See the six-month passport rule.
