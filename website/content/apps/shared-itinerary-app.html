The trouble with group trip plans

Group trips are planned in a chat and booked by several people. The flights are in one inbox, the villa in another, the restaurant booking is a screenshot someone posted on Tuesday. By the time you travel, nobody has the whole plan, and the group chat is 400 messages long.

A shared itinerary fixes that by giving the group one place where the bookings live, in date order, that everyone can open, even without signal.

How sharing works in Travary

  1. Start the trip

    Add a booking and the trip forms itself from its dates, or create it with a name and dates first.

  2. Invite the group

    Send a short code by text or chat. Up to six people can be on a trip, you included. They join from Trips in the app.

  3. Everyone adds what they booked

    Whoever books the dinner adds the dinner. You can edit and delete your own bookings; the organiser can edit anything.

  4. Everyone carries their own tickets

    Tickets on a shared trip are backed up and downloaded to each phone, so they open offline for whoever's at the front of the queue.

Private by design

You share with the people you invite, not with a public link, so the trip's dates and addresses don't travel further than the group. Passports and visas never leave each person's phone, even on a shared trip.

What the group sees each morning

Each day opens on its plan: the next thing with its ticket ready, the place you're staying that night, the tables booked. Swipe for tomorrow, or tap the date to see the whole trip on a calendar. Times are exactly as printed on each ticket, in local time, so a group arriving from different countries all see the same 7:30pm dinner.

Inside the trip, Travary spots what's missing: a night with nowhere booked, or no flight home for someone. And the trip's "Before you go" list holds the group's to-dos that aren't bookings, like who's collecting the hire car keys.

What it costs

Sharing a trip needs a Holiday Pass ([[price_pass]], a single payment for one trip of up to 30 days) or the Annual Pass ([[price_annual]] a year, for every trip you organise). Only the organiser needs one: a pass on a trip covers everyone on it. Each person's own trips, bookings and tickets are free.
