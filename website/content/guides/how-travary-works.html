1. Add a booking

Every trip starts with a booking: tap the plus and choose what it is, a flight, a stay, a ticket, a table, a hire car or just a note. Only a title and a date are ever required; times, references and details are there if you have them. Or pick a screenshot, photo or PDF of the confirmation and Smart Import reads it into a booking for you to check, with the dates and times highlighted. Nothing is saved until you've looked. The file you imported becomes the booking's ticket.

2. The trip forms itself

There's no trip to create first. Bookings group into trips by their dates, and the destination is read from the trip's name: call it "Japan holiday" or "Disney World" and Travary knows where you're going, and dresses the trip with that place's postcard and ticket art. If you prefer, start a trip yourself with a name and dates; Travary then keeps its bookings to those days, and asks before a booking stretches it.

3. Today opens on now

[[screen|today_lakes|Travary's Today screen on day 2 of a Lake District trip: a boat trip next up and the cottage's key card]]

During a trip, Travary opens on today. The greeting, then the day's cards down a time rail: the featured ticket in its place in time, your stay's key card after it, then the day's tables and tours, with finished things folding away under "Earlier". Swipe for tomorrow, or tap the date for the whole trip's calendar.

Times are exactly as printed on the ticket, in the local time where they happen. A dinner at 7:30pm in Rome stays 7:30pm, whatever time zone your phone was in when you booked it. (Why that matters.)

4. Between trips, it counts down

[[screen|countdown|Travary between trips: a Lake District postcard with a luggage tag reading 46 days to go]]

When you're not away, Today becomes a countdown to your next trip: that destination's postcard, as large as the screen allows, with a luggage tag counting the days. Every time you come back to the app, the postcard lifts in and the tag slaps on. If you want one for a trip before you've booked anything, the holiday countdown on this site makes one in your browser.

5. Inside a trip

Open a trip and its plan reads like a ledger, each booking on its date with its ticket on the row. What the trip still needs appears as dashed rows: nights without a stay, the flight out or home. Add it, or mark it "Not needed". Below that, Before you go is the trip's checklist for the jobs that aren't bookings, like the visa, the pet sitter or the travel money, each with an optional "by" date that can be weeks before the trip.

6. Documents and travel documents

[[screen|documents|Travary's Documents tab: every booking in the trip as a postage-stamp row under an Orlando stamp]]

The Documents tab gathers every ticket and booking in one place, as postage-stamp rows, all available offline. Separately, Travel documents holds passports, visas and insurance: stored only on your phone, behind its own Face ID, fingerprint or passcode, and locked again whenever you leave the app. They're never uploaded, never shared with the family, never sent to Smart Import, and their numbers stay masked until you ask. Each passport is checked against your next trip, and flagged if it has less than six months left after the trip ends.

7. Bring the family

With a Holiday Pass or the Annual Pass, share a trip with up to six people, you included, by sending them a short code. Everyone sees the same days on their own phone, iPhone or Android, adds the bookings they made, and carries their own tickets, backed up so they open on every phone. A pass on a trip covers everyone on it.
