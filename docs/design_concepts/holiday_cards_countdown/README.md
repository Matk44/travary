# Holiday cards and Today countdown — concept set

Generation mode: built-in ImageGen, reference-matched `ui-mockup` generation.

References used:

- Current Trips screen supplied by the product owner

- Approved Travary ticket, room-card, reservation-card, note-paper, and
  ongoing-tag assets

All three directions preserve the existing information hierarchy, real Flutter
text requirement, five-tab navigation, and raised selected-tab interaction.
The generated screens are design studies; final production assets must be
exported as blank raster shells and destination illustrations with Flutter
placing live text and hit targets over them.

## Concept A — Travel Folio

Prompt direction: turn each trip row into a slim stitched kraft
travel-document wallet with a cream live-copy label, navy elastic closure, and
a destination-specific die-cut luggage sticker. Current, upcoming, and past
states are expressed through opening/lift, pristine stock, and archival fading.
For Today, place a compact Lake District folio under the greeting: an oval
destination sticker plus a partially exposed cream departure card with a large
paper-printed `58` countdown.

Component language:

- Cards: kraft document wallets
- Destination image: die-cut luggage sticker
- Status: open/closed/archive treatment
- Tags: stitched cream labels and leather arrow tabs
- Icons: single-ink travel emblems printed on paper
- Motion: wallet lifts and card slides out; selected tab pops upward

## Concept B — Postcard Journey

Prompt direction: make every trip row a premium horizontal postcard. Use a
destination-specific vintage screen-print scene on the left and quiet cream
correspondence stock for live copy on the right, with blank postage-stamp
frames and text-free cancellation marks. For Today, use a clipped and taped
Lake District postcard with the scenic half beside a large `58` countdown.

Component language:

- Cards: deckled destination postcards
- Destination image: large scenic print panel
- Status: wax dot, clean future card, faded cancellation
- Tags: postage stamps and address rules
- Icons: perforated miniature stamps
- Motion: postcard corner lifts or slides upward; selected tab pops upward

## Concept C — Baggage Tag Collection

Prompt direction: make each trip row an oversized cream baggage label with a
compact cord or leather loop, aged-brass eyelet, destination illustration, and
blank tear-off status stub. For Today, use a wide baggage tag with a circular
destination emblem and two tactile flip-counter tiles reading `58`.

Component language:

- Cards: oversized baggage labels
- Destination image: one-colour screen print around the eyelet
- Status: coloured tear-off stub and cord material
- Tags: the card itself is the tag
- Icons: tiny single-ink suitcase/calendar emblems
- Motion: countdown tiles flip daily; selected tab pops upward

## Shared prompt constraints

Preserve Travary's warm linen, cream cotton paper, navy ink, teal/coral/sage/
mustard accents, risograph grain, aged brass, stitching, and soft contact
shadows. Avoid glossy gradients, glassmorphism, stock photography, flags,
logos, people, faces, watermarks, and generic Material colour tiles. Use
destination-specific imagery without changing navigation or information
architecture.
