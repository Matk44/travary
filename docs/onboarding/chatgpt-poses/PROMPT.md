# Travi key frames: one paste for ChatGPT

For the storyline in [`../STORYBOARD_V2.md`](../STORYBOARD_V2.md). Attach the
five images in this folder (image-1 … image-5) to one new ChatGPT message,
paste the prompt below, then reply "next" after each image until all 17 are
done. Save them as numbered (`01-empty.png` … `17-workshop.png`) in
`docs/mockups/onboarding-keyframes-v2/`.

If Travi starts to drift (shape, colours, size) after many images, start a
new chat with the same five images and the same paste, and say "start from
frame N".

```text
You're making the key frames for Travary's animated onboarding: a short
story where Travi, the coral ribbed-spinner suitcase mascot, helps the
traveller set up their holiday. Each image is the frame where one animated
shot ends and the next begins, so every image must line up exactly with the
others: same camera, same Travi, same spot. Travi is filmed on green and
the app places a different destination behind him.

I've attached 5 images:
- Image 1: the camera reference. ALWAYS edit Image 1. It sets the canvas
  (941 x 1672 portrait), camera, Travi's size and spot, the floor line, the
  folio and the green.
- Image 2: the last frame of Travi's arrival film (frames 2 and 17 only).
- Image 3: an approved storyboard frame (frame 12 only).
- Image 4: an earlier presenting pose (frame 13 only).
- Image 5: an earlier full-frame open folio (frame 7 only).
Images 2-5 are references only for the frames that name them. Never copy
their camera, size, position, background or scenery.

Rules for frames 1-16:
- Same canvas, camera, scale, lighting and folded-paper craft look as Image 1.
- Travi is the same character every time: coral ribbed spinner suitcase,
  navy trim and corner caps, gold telescopic handle with a navy grip, navy
  top carry handle, black wheels with gold hubs, a cream luggage tag with a
  navy palm tree hanging from his handle (hanging the same way every time),
  big friendly cartoon eyes and brows, folded-paper hands.
- Travi stands exactly where he is in Image 1: same size, same position,
  wheels on the same floor line. Only his arms, face and what he holds
  change.
- The navy holiday folio (palm emblem, brass clasp, gold corners) stands at
  the left as in Image 1, unless the frame says Travi holds it.
- The background is a plain, evenly lit chroma green (about #00B140) with
  nothing else in it: no floor, horizon, shadow, reflection, gradient,
  vignette or scenery. No green tones or green spill on Travi or the props.
- Match Image 1 by eye. Small differences in the green, the folio or
  Travi's exact pixels are fine: they're cleaned up afterwards. Never use
  pixel editing, never stop to ask, and only redo a frame if Travi's design,
  size or position is clearly wrong.
- Anything Travi holds that the app will write on (tags, cards, paper,
  envelopes) is completely blank: no text, numbers, logos, lines or symbols.
- No text, labels, UI, buttons, prices, logos or watermark anywhere.
- Crisp edges, no motion blur.

Make ONE image per reply, in this order. Start with frame 1 now. When I say
"next", make the following frame.

1. empty: no Travi at all. Only the folio, standing at the left exactly as
   in Image 1, on the green. (He rolls in from here.)

2. hello: copy Travi's pose and expression from Image 2 exactly: left arm
   reaching out to the side with an open hand, right arm raised in a wave,
   the same friendly closed-mouth smile, eyes on the viewer. Hands empty.
   No workshop, arches or tickets.

3. wow: the world has just appeared behind him. Both arms flung up and
   open, eyes wide and sparkling, mouth open in a delighted gasp, leaning
   back a touch on his wheels (wheels still on the floor line). Hands empty.

4. tag: Travi proudly holds up a large blank departure luggage tag in front
   of his chest with both hands, facing the viewer squarely so it can be
   written on. Cream card, a rounded rectangle about 60% of his body width,
   a brass grommet hole at the top and a short loop of twine. Perfectly
   blank. He looks at the viewer, excited.

5. toss: he has just flicked something toward the viewer. His right arm
   follows through, reaching toward the camera with an open hand; his left
   arm is out for balance; a cheeky, pleased grin. Hands empty: whatever he
   threw has already left the frame.

6. folio-open: Travi holds the navy folio himself, open toward the viewer
   with both hands like presenting a fresh notebook: its two inside pages
   are blank cream paper, with a small paper pocket on the left page. Front
   on, about as wide as his body, at chest height. No folio standing at the
   left. Warm, inviting smile.

7. folio-full: like Image 5: the open navy folio fills almost the whole
   frame, its pages blank cream paper, held up by Travi, whose eyes, handle
   and paper hands peek over its top edge. Same folio size and position as
   Image 5. Travi follows the character rules above.

8. reading: Travi studies a blank sheet of cream paper held up in one hand,
   a small brass magnifying glass in the other, one eyebrow raised in
   concentration, the tip of his tongue out.

9. card: Travi holds up a blank cream booking card (like a large postcard
   with rounded corners) in both hands in front of his chest, facing the
   viewer squarely so it can be written on, beaming with pride.

10. proud: hands resting on his sides like hands on hips, chest out, a warm
    satisfied smile, eyes on the viewer: "job done". Hands empty.

11. envelope: the same pose as Image 1, but instead of the tag he offers a
    sealed blank cream envelope with a navy wax seal toward the viewer.

12. linked: match the acting of Image 3: Travi tucks a cream identity tag
    (a small person silhouette is the only mark on it) into the top of the
    navy folio, which he holds against his side; eyes closed in a happy,
    proud smile. No folio standing at the left.

13. present: the same as Image 4: Travi presenting with an open palm toward
    the floor in front of him. But nothing at all on the floor in front of
    him: no passes, no tickets, no bar.

14. cheer: a burst of joy: both paper fists punched up high, eyes squeezed
    shut in a huge grin, wheels on the floor line.

15. wave: Travi waves goodbye with his right arm held high, and with his
    left hand holds the closed navy folio out toward the viewer as if
    handing it over. Joyful "have a wonderful trip" face. No folio standing
    at the left.

16. oops: a sheepish shrug, one hand rubbing the back of his handle, a
    small apologetic smile and raised brows.

17. workshop: something different: a background, not a pose, and not on
    green. Edit Image 2: remove Travi, the folio and both tickets completely,
    leaving the empty paper workshop: the petrol-teal paper wall, the curved
    paper-theatre arches on both sides and the cream paper floor, with the
    same warm lighting. Make it taller than Image 2 by extending the wall
    upward, so the floor meets the wall about two thirds of the way down.
    Keep the upper wall and the middle of the floor calm and uncluttered.
    No characters, props or text.
```
