# Travi cameos: one paste for ChatGPT

Travi's small appearances around the app after onboarding (plan in
[`../HANDOVER_2026-10-10.md`](../HANDOVER_2026-10-10.md), "Travi cameos").
Attach the two images in this folder (image-1, image-2) to one new ChatGPT
message, paste the prompt below, and let it make all seven. Save them as
named in `docs/mockups/travi-cameos-v1/`.

Two formats:
- **Peek** (frames 1–6): Travi pops up from behind something in the app
  (the countdown postcard, Today's header). The bottom edge of the image is
  that edge: everything below it is hidden in the app.
- **Lounge** (frame 7): the free day, full body in a deckchair.

```text
You're making 7 small "cameo" images of Travi, the coral ribbed-spinner
suitcase mascot of the Travary travel app. They become the start and end
frames of short animated clips: Travi is filmed on green screen and the app
places him over its own screens, so the green must be plain and the frames
in each format must line up with each other.

I've attached 2 images:
- Image 1: Travi as he looks (identity, colours, craft style, his luggage
  tag). Copy the character exactly, but not this image's framing.
- Image 2: his cheer pose (frame 3 only).

Rules for every image:
- Square canvas, 1024 x 1024.
- Travi is the same character every time: coral ribbed spinner suitcase,
  navy trim and corner caps, gold telescopic handle with a navy grip, navy
  top carry handle, black wheels with gold hubs, a cream luggage tag with a
  navy palm tree hanging from his handle, big friendly cartoon eyes and
  brows, folded-paper hands. Tactile folded-paper craft look, warm soft
  light, as in Image 1.
- The background is a plain, evenly lit chroma green (about #00B140) with
  nothing else in it: no floor, horizon, shadow, gradient or scenery. No
  green tones or green spill on Travi or the props.
- Anything he holds that the app could write on (boarding pass, card) is
  completely blank: no text, numbers, logos or lines.
- No text, labels, UI, logos or watermark anywhere.
- Small differences in the green or in exact pixels are fine: they're
  cleaned up afterwards. Never use pixel editing, never stop to ask, and
  make all seven one after another.

PEEK FORMAT (frames 1-6): Travi is peeking up from behind a wall that is the
bottom edge of the image. He is centred left to right and fairly large:
his body is about 560 px wide, the top of his telescopic handle about 140 px
from the top of the image. The bottom edge of the image cuts straight across
his body a little below his mouth, so we see his handle, his face, his tag
and his paper hands; the rest of him (and his wheels) is hidden below the
edge. Keep Travi in exactly the same place and size in all six; only his
arms, face and what he holds change.

1. peek-hello: peeking up happily, both paper hands resting on the bottom
   edge as if leaning on the top of a wall, bright eyes on the viewer, warm
   closed-mouth smile.

2. peek-wave: the same, but one paper hand is raised in a cheerful wave,
   the other still resting on the edge. Open happy smile.

3. peek-cheer: like Image 2's acting: both paper arms flung up high in
   delight, eyes squeezed shut in a huge grin.

4. peek-packing: holding up a folded coral-striped swimsuit in one hand and
   a small cream sunhat in the other, looking pleased and busy: "packing!"

5. peek-ready: wearing round navy sunglasses and the cream sunhat, giving a
   big thumbs-up with one hand, the other hand resting on the edge, grinning.

6. peek-today: wearing the sunglasses, holding up a blank cream boarding
   pass (perfectly blank) with both hands, mouth open in excitement: "it's
   today!"

LOUNGE FORMAT (frame 7):

7. lounge: Travi's whole body, about 70% of the image's height, lying back
   in a coral-and-cream striped folded-paper deckchair, seen from the front
   at a slight angle. Round navy sunglasses on, one paper hand behind his
   head, the other holding a small coconut drink with a paper umbrella.
   Relaxed, contented, eyes half-closed behind the sunglasses, soft smile.
   His wheels rest on the chair's footrest. The chair stands on the bottom
   of the image; nothing else is in the scene.
```

Save as `peek-hello.png`, `peek-wave.png`, `peek-cheer.png`,
`peek-packing.png`, `peek-ready.png`, `peek-today.png`, `lounge.png`.
