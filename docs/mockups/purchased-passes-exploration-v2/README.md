## Final prompts

### 1. Storybook golden ticket

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: alternative miniature Holiday Pass ticket badge for Travary, a warm tactile vintage travel organiser.
Primary request: a magical golden ticket inspired by Charlie and the Chocolate Factory's celebrated golden ticket, adapted to a holiday boarding pass. Capture the delight of finding a precious thin GOLD FOIL ticket inside a chocolate wrapper: delicate crinkled metallic foil, charming ornate dark letterpress typography, fine Victorian engraved flourishes and an elegant ornamental frame. It should feel whimsical, storybook and inviting.
Subject: one horizontal rectangular gold foil ticket, approximately 2.5:1 width to height. Slightly irregular thin foil edges and modest inward semicircular cuts at the two short ends. The large central title is two lines, "HOLIDAY" then "PASS", in rich dark chocolate-black ornate yet very readable vintage serif lettering. One small but clear engraved airplane silhouette sits to the left of the title, integrated into the frame. Decorations are confined to the outer edges, leaving generous clean gold around the large title.
Materials: real thin yellow gold foil pressed onto paper, subtle folds, soft wrinkles, warm amber-to-champagne reflected light, fine printed dark brown engraved border. Flat thin foil rather than a thick cast metal object. No thick beveled metallic rim.
Composition: straight overhead view, level and symmetrical, whole ticket visible, centered and occupying most of a wide canvas with small clear margins. GENUINELY TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND and clean alpha cutout. Shallow attached edge shadow only.
Text (verbatim): "HOLIDAY" and "PASS". No other words, no Wonka branding, no film names, no small print.
Constraints: recognizable and legible as a badge about 120px wide in an app. Strong silhouette and clear title. No chocolate, wrapper, hands, props, floating sparkles, scene, background, watermark or perspective tilt. Single ticket only.
```

### 3. Vintage airline

```text
Use case: stylized-concept

Primary request: a beautiful collectible mid-century airline boarding ticket that feels printed on paper, with selective gold foil, to compare against an entirely gold ticket.

Text (verbatim): "HOLIDAY" on the first line, "PASS" on the second. No other wording, no tiny decorative text or barcode.
```

### 4. Modern satin gold

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: alternative Holiday Pass purchased-status badge for Travary.
Primary request: a modern, beautifully restrained satin-gold boarding ticket. The quiet luxury alternative to ornate vintage designs; still a tangible tactile object suited to a warm travel scrapbook app.

Simple softly rounded corners, one precise half-circle notch cut into the center of each short end, one fine vertical row of perforation dots splitting off the left quarter. A bold simple dark navy airplane silhouette occupies the left quarter, pointed up and to the right. The right area has large "HOLIDAY" above "PASS" in beautifully spaced, weighty contemporary sans-serif capitals, midnight navy ink, extremely legible. Gold is warm and luminous but not mirror-chrome. Minimal embossing, shallow paper edge and barely-there attached shadow. No thick raised border. No dramatic wrinkles.
Composition: straight overhead, flat and level, centered and filling most of a wide canvas, entire silhouette visible with small margins. Genuinely transparent background with clean alpha.
Text (verbatim): "HOLIDAY" and "PASS"; no other text.
Constraints: must work at 120px wide in a real app; one ticket only. No vintage scrolls, ornate letters, winged emblems, rays, badges, tiny text, barcode, stars, props, hands, scene, background, watermark or perspective tilt.
```
