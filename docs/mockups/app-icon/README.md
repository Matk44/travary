# Travary app icon concepts

Four exploratory app-icon directions generated for storefront recognition testing. These are concept masters only; they do not replace the current iOS or Android launcher assets.

Shared production constraints:

- 1024 × 1024 full-square artwork; no pre-rounded outer mask.
- One dominant travel-stationery symbol with a clear small-size silhouette.
- Travary palette: deep petrol teal, ink navy, warm cream, coral, and restrained brass.
- Tactile linen, cotton paper, screen-print ink, and brass are used as subtle material cues.
- No words, tiny labels, flags, globes, generic location pins, or detailed interface replicas.
- Primary content remains inside a generous mask-safe area.

Prompts and final concept notes will be recorded here after generation.

## Research-derived design strategy

- Apple recommends a simple, unique core idea with minimal shapes, centered primary content, illustrations rather than photos, and square unmasked source art.
- Google Play requires a full-square 512 px PNG for the store listing and applies its own corner mask and shadow.
- Feature contrast helps an item separate from surrounding alternatives in visual search.
- Processing fluency research links fast, easy visual processing with more positive aesthetic response.
- Logo research found that recognition and positive image benefit from harmony, naturalness, and only moderate elaboration.

These findings led to the shared rule: one tactile object, one bold silhouette, one coral attention cue, and no small typography or destination montage.

## Concepts

### 01 — Sunrise luggage tag

An oversized cream luggage tag on petrol-teal textile, carrying a coral sunrise and one broad navy journey line. This is the strongest link to Travary’s existing hanging-tag interface and physical asset system.

### 02 — Postage window

A collectible cream stamp framing a teal field, coral destination sun, and cream journey path. It carries the postcard language most directly, though the perforations and postmark lose some definition at very small sizes.

### 03 — Golden ticket sun

A coral ticket silhouette, cream sun, and teal compass star on navy woven cloth. This has the strongest small-size contrast and the clearest App Store / Play Store grid presence.

### 04 — Folded journey T

Two cream ticket strips form a tactile Travary “T,” pinned by a coral seal and brass stud. It is the most ownable brand monogram, but the narrow vertical form creates less visual mass than Concept 03.

## Small-size review

At 64 px and 32 px, Concept 03 retains the clearest silhouette and colour hierarchy. Concept 01 is the best alternate when continuity with the in-app luggage-tag metaphor is weighted above maximum store-grid impact.

## Generation prompt record

All four concepts were generated as square, edge-to-edge raster artwork using these common constraints:

- Use the current Travary palette and tactile cotton-paper / woven-textile material language.
- Preserve a generous platform-mask-safe zone.
- Do not pre-round or mask the square exterior.
- Avoid tiny labels, flags, globes, generic location pins, airplanes, detailed scenes, and interface replicas.
- Limit the composition to three major shapes plus restrained material details.

The concept-specific prompts respectively requested:

1. A cream luggage tag, brass eyelet, coral sunrise, and broad navy route gesture.
2. A scalloped cream postage stamp, teal field, coral sun, and simple cream route.
3. A coral admission ticket, cream sun, teal compass star, and tiny mustard hub.
4. A paper-strip “T,” coral seal, brass stud, and one navy debossed route line.
