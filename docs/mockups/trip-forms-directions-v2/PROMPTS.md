# Travary clean screen directions — round 2

Created 5 October 2026 with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## User feedback

The first boards felt too busy. The revised brief is catchy, clean, simple UI/UX that does not overwhelm the user.

## Design decisions

- One focal point and one primary action per screen.
- Plain warm ivory, navy editorial type, minimal rules and restrained illustration.
- Trip opens on its plan; people, style and settings move behind Trip details.
- Add sheet starts with import and four common manual types; remaining kinds sit behind More booking types.
- Stay form shows the essential fields; references, guests, attachments and notes sit behind More details.
- Flight search shows one result; the editable review follows Use this flight.
- Secondary trip/form pages use back navigation, leaving out the five fabric tabs.
- No duplicated preview above the form and no decorative object collection.

## Inputs

- Earlier rejected board: `../trip-forms-directions-v1/03-quiet-ledger.png`
- Destination illustration: `../../../art_packs/generated/trip_postcards/jp.png`

All booking, property and flight information is illustrative. Production inputs and text remain live UI.

```text
Label "Location", value "Kyoto, Japan" on a hairline.
One row with two equal date controls, label "Check-in" / value "12 Oct 2026", label "Check-out" / value "14 Oct 2026". Small clean calendar outlines optional. Dates are normal crisp live text, NO rubber stamp texture.
A small quiet "2 nights" under the date pair.
One collapsed link "More details ›" for room, reference, guests, notes and attachments; none of those fields visible.
Then ample plain space before one wide navy "Add stay" action anchored near bottom. The form stays coherent, aligned, with enough room to scan each field and comfortable touch heights. Never show a giant hotel key card, luggage fob, separate property summary, paperclip, or trip postcard selector on this form.

Screen 4 caption "Find your flight".
Simple back arrow, serif title "Find your flight", small context "Japan · 11–30 Oct".
Essential search controls: hairline field label "Flight number" value "QF25"; date control label "Departure date" value "11 Oct 2026". Two fields only, ample vertical separation.
Below a single crisp flat navy RESULT PANEL, moderate height (about 150 phone pixels), no shadow. A small uppercase "FLIGHT FOUND", prominent "QF25", clean route "Sydney → Tokyo", and one metadata line "11 Oct · 20:55". It is a quiet nod to a departures board: at most one subtle midline in the flight number, no full grids of giant individual letters, no destination stamp, no boarding-pass preview, no duplicate route form below.
One navy primary "Use this flight" action near bottom (app opens an editable review after tapping); a quiet text link "Enter flight manually" below. ONE result and one primary action. Plenty of ivory space.

Hard avoid: linen texture, leather, stitching, brass rings, twine, tags, perforated form borders, frayed paper edges, embossed empty tickets, airport logos, barcodes, large illustrative booking toys, repeated scenic art, two cards per form, multiple primary buttons, all optional fields shown at once, fixed tab bars on these secondary screens. Do not reproduce the crowded composition of Image 1. Do not add extra explanatory text or promotional wording. No tiny labels or low-contrast text. All booking and flight data are illustrative.
Direction-specific style:
Board title "D · Postcard light"; small subtitle "One beautiful destination. A calm, focused interface."
The trip screen's single illustration is a beautiful compact landscape window about 145 phone pixels tall directly under the title and date line. It has clean rounded corners, NO physical postcard border, and a simplified screen-print Mount Fuji with one pagoda silhouette and coral sun; fewer leaves and no dense grain. This is the only colour-rich art on the entire board. The other screens use crisp navy Source Serif 4 headings, Inter labels and simple navy icons. Keep a barely noticeable warm paper quality only in the flat base colour, not actual visible fibres or fabric. Buttons are flat navy with a tiny optional ticket-notch detail. The result panel is deep teal-navy. 35 percent calm blank space per form. This direction is artful, warm, premium, distinctive and immediately much cleaner than Image 1.
```
