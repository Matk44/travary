# Travary retro travel icon exploration

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool, using the app's existing artwork as the visual brief. These are exploratory icon candidates; no launcher assets or app code were changed.

## References inspected

- `docs/mockups/countdown-art-review-v2/in-app-proposal.png` — current paper, typography and component context.
- `docs/design_concepts/theme_aligned_postcard/today_countdown.png` — restrained use of scenic art in the app UI.
- `art_packs/sources/city_fr_paris/common/countdown_postcard.png` and `art_packs/sources/city_jp_kyoto/common/trip_postcard.png` — layered postcard scenes and distressed print edges.
- `assets/art/flight/any_plane_01_scene.png` — petrol, coral and cream travel-poster printing.

## Candidates

- **08 — Postcard window:** [1024 px icon](08-postcard-window.png) · [64 px](08-postcard-window-64.png) · [32 px](08-postcard-window-32.png). Strongest match to the in-app postcards; the window and coral sky survive at small size, but the image reads as scenery more than a brand mark.
- **09 — Poster monogram:** [1024 px icon](09-poster-monogram.png) · [64 px](09-poster-monogram-64.png) · [32 px](09-poster-monogram-32.png). Stronger brand recognition at small size. The T encloses a vintage travel scene and winding path. The glow from the first generation was removed in a targeted edit.

Both final icons are full-square opaque PNGs, 1024 × 1024. Generated 1254 px source images are retained alongside the exports. These have not been tested for install conversion.

## Exact prompts

### 08 — Postcard window

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: Travary square app icon concept, full-bleed raster artwork.
Brand context: Travary is a family holiday companion. Its existing artwork is rich, nostalgic mid-century travel-poster illustration printed on textured paper: petrol teal and ink navy scenery, warm ivory, coral evening light, a little muted mustard, layered screen-printed shapes and subtle imperfect ink grain. Match this established house style precisely.
Primary request: create a compact, memorable illustrated icon that feels like a crop of Travary's destination postcards. A SINGLE bold old-fashioned arched travel window acts as the graphic silhouette; through it is the promise of the next holiday, with layered deep teal landscape, a winding ivory road and a large coral sunset sky. The window is warm ivory printed paper, the scenery is two-dimensional illustrated ink. The composition should feel artist-drawn for a premium 1950s travel poster, with confident shapes and emotional atmosphere.
Scene/backdrop: full-square opaque deep navy paper with fine authentic screen-print texture, visible to all four edges.
Composition: the arched window fills about 80% of square and is slightly asymmetrical, with its dark teal landscape shape reaching forward at lower left and a clear coral sky spanning upper right. The road is broad and leads inward. Three to five broad colour areas; detail in print texture only. Hold at 32px. Cropped like a strong poster, no little icon floating in empty space.
Style and materials: mid-century lithograph and screen print, matte imperfect ink on aged but clean paper, slight registration variation and soft grain, elegant editorial illustration. Strong contrast, no glossy effects or 3D.
Constraints: Original standalone artwork; no references to a specific place, landmark or country. No words, numbers, letters, fake text, labels, stamp perforations, luggage tag, ticket, compass, globe, map pin, airplane, check mark, device mockup, 3D, shiny enamel, gradient, neon, photographic scene, watermark or decorative border. Do not pre-round outer square.
```

### 09 — Poster monogram

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: Travary square app icon concept, full-bleed raster artwork.
Brand context: The Travary in-app artwork is beautifully printed vintage travel-poster art: rich petrol-teal and navy landscape layers, coral and apricot sunset, warm cream, subtle risograph grain and ink on cotton paper. Create a brand mark that belongs next to those existing postcards.
Primary request: an art-directed retro travel emblem whose hero is a CUSTOM, hugely drawn capital letter T, integrated with a journey landscape. The T is not plain typography: its wide horizontal top is a broad coral evening sky band and its descending stem becomes a wide sunlit cream trail flowing through two layered teal hills. The silhouette reads clearly and immediately as a T from 32px, while the route within it carries holiday anticipation. Keep this an illustration with print character, not a sterile logo or a detailed painting.
Scene/backdrop: opaque full-square ink navy paper texture extending all the way to the four square edges.
Composition: oversized T occupies 78% of canvas, nearly poster-like crop, bold upper bar and sturdy central stem; one coral sun peeks from behind the T's top right edge, partially occluded and secondary. Broad warm cream trail and teal hill layers create the form. Distinctive negative space and visually balanced shape. Rich but limited screen-print colour: navy #1C2A47, teal #1E5B70, warm cream #F8F4EC, coral #E5785B, restrained apricot.
Style and materials: lovingly art-directed 1950s travel-poster silkscreen, analogue ink texture, slight hand-cut edge character, mature editorial taste; sharp enough for small-size use.
Constraints: One emblem only. No words or other lettering, numbers, fake text, labels, ticket, postage stamp, luggage tag, brass, compass, globe, map pin, airplane, check mark, 3D, gloss, plastic, neon, gradients, photographic scene, watermark, device mockup or decorative border. Do not pre-round the square. Must look like Travary's existing printed travel posters, never a generic AI image.
```

### 09 — Targeted edit

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the attached Travary illustrated T icon. Change only the bright hazy outer glow around the T. Replace it with a crisp, matte printed edge and very subtle uneven warm-cream ink registration on the navy paper, matching the original mid-century screen-print texture. Keep the T silhouette, all internal landscapes, coral sky, ivory road, navy full-square background, overall crop, proportions and palette unchanged. No light bloom, neon, shiny finish, text, extra objects or decorative border.
```
