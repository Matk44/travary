“A · TRAVI PORTRAIT”
“B · TICKET HERITAGE”

This is a warm premium travel brand for adults and families: cheerful, crafted and sophisticated, not a baby cartoon or game character. Use a retracted pull handle / short grab handle appropriate to each icon. Reduce illustration detail for clarity. No arms or extended telescopic rails in these icons.

A · TRAVI PORTRAIT:
A bold close-up of Travi on a simple deep navy background. Three-quarter front view, slight lively tilt of about five degrees. No airplane, no perforation dashes or fine borders. Rich cut-paper depth and quiet grain. This is the most heritage-oriented direction; the ticket must not make the icon crowded.

C · TRAVI MARK:
A deliberately distilled graphic emblem of the SAME Travi luggage identity on a simple petrol-teal #1E5B70 background. Shell with handle occupies about 72 percent of the icon height and about 64 percent of the width. Balanced centred layout and roomy corners. No arms, wheels, tags, brass rivets or thin line details. A beautiful premium paper-relief brand mark: slightly dimensional, bold simple shapes, restrained grain, much simpler than A while clearly recognisable as coral Travi. Do not turn it into an unrelated flat generic suitcase or a bag with a pocket.

Art direction overall: dimensional handmade paper theatre, sunbaked coral, ink navy, warm ivory and petrol teal; editorial warmth, clean silhouette, controlled highlights and soft interior shadows. Backgrounds inside the icons are simple and full bleed. Keep lively open eyes in all three. Critical face and handles stay away from icon corners. No text inside icons, logos from other brands, full holiday landscapes, mountains, glossy plastic, photorealism, chrome, stars, confetti, sleepiness, front zip pockets, arms or exaggerated childlike eyes. Make the board feel like serious, beautiful brand exploration rather than onboarding screenshots.
