## Comparison board

1. **Classic:** full Travi on the cream ticket, with restrained diagonal corner stripes. Closest to the approved starting point.
2. **Hello Travi:** larger face and tilted close-up; the suitcase extends beyond the bottom of the ticket. The most immediate expression, with less of the full luggage body visible.
3. **Ticket Tilt:** full upright Travi with the cream ticket rotated behind him. Adds a sense of movement without changing his pose.
4. **Ink Ticket:** navy ticket with a bold cream outline, recalling the original icon's treatment. The darker framing makes this the most restrained option.
5. **Boarding Stub:** full Travi over a tear-off stub and perforation line. Strong ticket storytelling, but the stub gives the character less space.
6. **Stripe Sweep:** larger near-front-facing Travi with two diagonal ribbons behind the lower shell. Combines a complete suitcase, a more prominent face and the original stripe motif.

## Recommendation for discussion

My shortlist is **6 — Stripe Sweep** and **2 — Hello Travi**, with **6** my first choice. Its full luggage silhouette and prominent happy face balance the mascot and ticket heritage. **2** is the stronger choice if the expressive close-up matters most. These are visual judgements from the board, not measured recognition results or a final owner decision.
