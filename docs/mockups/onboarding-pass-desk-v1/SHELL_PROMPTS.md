```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: reusable blank transparent paper pass shell for Travary's native onboarding; all type, price and selection controls will be live native overlays.
Input image 1 is a STYLE AND MATERIAL reference only. Extract the visual identity of ONE of its physical paper passes, but do not copy any writing, icon, palm tree, check mark or radio button.
Primary request: a single flat front-facing HORIZONTAL ticket/pass, about four times wider than it is tall, centred on a landscape transparent canvas. Fill almost the complete canvas with the full silhouette and a small soft contact shadow. Request a wide canvas about 1536 by 512 pixels; preserve the entire ticket with 4–6% side padding and only the vertical transparent space needed. Warm cream gently fibrous cardstock body, short coloured tear-off stub on the LEFT occupying exactly 22% of the ticket width, realistic small round side notches at the edges, subtle irregular hand-cut corners, fine perforated tear seam between the stub and body, one small brass eyelet at lower left. Rich embossed folded-paper material matching the reference. The right 78% remains an absolutely clean, quiet cream text space. The left stub is also BLANK. Straight camera orthogonal to the card, no perspective tilt. Warm upper-left light, physically believable thin thickness and modest bottom-right shadow.
Constraints: true transparent alpha outside the ticket. Complete object inside frame; no scenery, multiple tickets, mascot, hand, labels, text of any size, logos, symbols, numbers, watermark, barcode, palm-tree motif, prices, status or check marks. Do not flatten onto a background or draw a checkerboard.
The left tear-off stub is rich CORAL orange with tactile paper grain. The body is cream; brass eyelet.
```
