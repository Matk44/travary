# Empty Today countdown concepts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen workflow using the approved Travary Today, Trips, countdown, woven-background, and tactile-navigation references.

Shared scenario:

- today contains no itinerary items;
- the next item in Trips is Lake District, 25–28 November 2026;
- the next trip starts in 58 days, lasts 4 days, and has 3 bookings;
- Today remains the selected home-navigation tab;
- the destination hero is supplied by the resolved theme or downloadable destination pack;
- Flutter renders all live text, countdown values, buttons, and hit targets over generated raster shells.

## 01 — Split Postcard

A large postcard divides the destination hero and countdown into stable left/right live zones. It is the clearest, most responsive, and closest to the previously approved countdown direction.

## 02 — Hanging Luggage Tag

The destination poster becomes the dominant hero, with a large readable countdown tag tied over one corner. It gives downloadable destination art maximum visual impact.

## 03 — Tear-off Calendar

A landscape destination poster is physically bound to a large countdown page. It makes anticipation the emotional centre and creates a natural daily page-change animation opportunity.

## 04 — Departure Folio

A destination postcard and perforated countdown ticket sit inside a stitched travel folio. It is the richest tactile object and provides strong fixed zones for trip facts and a View trip action.
