# Documents ticket asset prompts

Generated with the built-in ImageGen workflow using the approved Documents
mockup, existing Travary paper shells, linen texture, and the current booking
scene library as references.

## Shared ticket-shell prompt contract

- One isolated, straight-on horizontal ticket with all edges visible.
- Warm ivory cotton paper with subtle fibres and a minimal contact shadow.
- Blank date, icon, title, and metadata zones.
- Date divider at about 14% and scene perforation at about 69%.
- Genuine transparency outside the ticket and inside the framed right-hand
  scene window.
- No scenery, text, pseudo-text, numbers, icons, barcode, logo, device mockup,
  perspective, rotation, crop, or watermark.
- Three variants: classic scalloped admission slip, half-moon railway coupon,
  and restrained deckled cinema voucher.

The generated shells were mechanically normalized to 1983 × 354 while
preserving alpha.

## Shared scenic-panel prompt contract

- Edge-to-edge 1536 × 1024 landscape illustration.
- Mid-century travel-poster composition, flat layered screen-print shapes,
  restrained risograph grain, and the established Travary art style.
- Deep petrol teal/navy, muted sage, warm ivory, coral sunset accents, and
  restrained mustard details.
- Main subject stays inside the central 65% safe area so the image survives a
  wide ticket-window crop.
- No ticket border, text, numbers, logo, people, photorealism, gradients, or
  watermark.

Generated subjects:

- Tropical waterside lunch terrace with three hanging lanterns, intimate table,
  lagoon, distant hills, coral sun, hibiscus, and palms.
- Open-sided thatched lagoon grill with counter, warm lanterns, hearth glow,
  water, tropical foliage, and coral sun.
- Refined coral, ivory, and mustard fireworks above a deep navy tropical beach,
  with reflections across a teal lagoon.
- True flat-bottom airboat with large rear propeller cage moving through
  mangroves and sawgrass at golden hour, accompanied by a white egret.
