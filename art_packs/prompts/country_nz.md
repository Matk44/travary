# New Zealand destination art pack

Built-in ImageGen, reference-matched generation mode. The strict Travary screen
and matching Travary shells supplied geometry, tactile paper, shadows and
live-text zones. Palette: fern green, charcoal, warm ivory, glacial blue, red
ochre and tussock gold.

## Featured ticket

Generate a transparent 2:1 Travary ticket with a calm fern-green left 42%, and
a dramatic fiord, waterfalls, snow peaks, glacial water, silver fern, red
tussock and small lighthouse on the right. Include the detachable ivory barcode
stub and a fern/glacial-blue/ochre/gold landscape-pattern strip. No people,
cultural motifs, text, numbers, flags, branding or watermark.

## Room card

Generate a transparent 2:1 ivory accommodation card with the left 62% blank.
Restrict a timber lodge window, glacial lake, mountains, waterfall, silver fern
and tussock to the right; finish with a fern-green, blue and ochre landscape
stripe. No text, numbers, barcode, branding or watermark.

## Reservation card

Generate a transparent 2.5:1 ivory reservation shell with cream cord, brass
grommet and completely blank fern-green luggage tag. Keep the center-left
empty. Add a gold divider and compact fiord, waterfall, snow peaks, lighthouse,
fern and tussock vignette on the far right. No embedded tag icon, text,
numbers, branding or watermark.
