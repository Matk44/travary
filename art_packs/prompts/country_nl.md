# Netherlands destination art pack

Built-in ImageGen, reference-matched generation mode. The strict Travary screen
and matching Travary shells supplied geometry, tactile paper, shadows and
live-text zones. Palette: Delft navy, royal orange, warm ivory, tile blue,
tulip red, meadow green and soft gold.

## Featured ticket

Generate a transparent 2:1 Travary ticket with a calm Delft-navy left 42%, and
gabled canal houses, arched bridge, bicycle, windmill, tulip fields and water
reflections on the right. Include the detachable ivory barcode stub and a
royal-orange/Delft-blue/tulip-red tile-pattern strip. No people, text, numbers,
flags, branding or watermark.

## Room card

Generate a transparent 2:1 ivory accommodation card with the left 62% blank.
Restrict a canal-house window, gabled façades, arched bridge, reflections,
tulips and distant windmill to the right; finish with an orange, blue and red
Delft-inspired tile stripe. No text, numbers, barcode, branding or watermark.

## Reservation card

Generate a transparent 2.5:1 ivory reservation shell with cream cord, brass
grommet and completely blank royal-orange luggage tag. Keep the center-left
empty. Add a Delft-blue divider and compact canal houses, bridge, bicycle,
windmill, tulips and reflection vignette on the far right. No embedded tag
icon, text, numbers, branding or watermark.
