# Canada destination art pack

Built-in ImageGen, reference-matched generation mode. The strict Travary screen
and an existing Travary shell supply geometry, tactile paper, shadows and
live-text zones. Palette: midnight navy, maple red, warm ivory, alpine teal,
pine green and golden ochre.

## Featured ticket

Generate a transparent 2:1 Travary ticket with a quiet midnight-blue left 42%,
and turquoise alpine lake, Rocky Mountain peaks, evergreen forest, red canoe,
distant waterfront skyline and restrained maple ornament on the right. Include
the detachable ivory barcode stub and maple-red/navy/gold woven security strip.
No text, numbers, flags, branding or watermark.

## Room card

Generate a transparent 2:1 ivory accommodation card with the left 62% blank.
Restrict a timber lodge window, turquoise lake, snowy peaks, forest and maple
leaves to the right; finish with a maple-red, navy, alpine-teal and ochre woven
stripe. No text, numbers, barcode, branding or watermark.

## Reservation card

Generate a transparent 2.5:1 ivory reservation shell with cream cord, brass
grommet and completely blank maple-red luggage tag. Keep the center-left empty.
Add an ochre divider and a compact mountain, lake, pine, canoe, maple and
waterfront vignette on the far right. No embedded tag icon, text, numbers,
branding or watermark.
