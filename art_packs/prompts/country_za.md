# South Africa destination art pack

Built-in ImageGen, reference-matched generation mode. The strict Travary screen
and an existing Travary shell supply geometry, tactile paper, shadows and
live-text zones. Palette: deep blue-green, protea coral, warm ivory, ochre,
coastal turquoise, fynbos green and ink navy.

## Featured ticket

Generate a transparent 2:1 Travary ticket with a quiet blue-green left 42%,
and a flat-topped Cape mountain, turquoise coast, Cape Dutch vineyard, protea,
ochre grassland and subtle distant elephant on the right. Include the
detachable ivory barcode stub and a protea-coral/ochre/teal woven strip. No
text, numbers, flags, branding or watermark.

## Room card

Generate a transparent 2:1 ivory accommodation card with the left 62% blank.
Restrict a Cape Dutch arched window, vineyard, flat-topped mountain, protea and
fynbos to the right; finish with a deep-teal, coral, ochre and navy woven
stripe. No text, numbers, barcode, branding or watermark.

## Reservation card

Generate a transparent 2.5:1 ivory reservation shell with cream cord, brass
grommet and a completely blank ochre luggage tag. Keep the center-left empty.
Add a deep-teal divider and compact mountain, coast, vineyard, protea, fynbos
and subtle elephant vignette on the far right. No embedded tag icon, text,
numbers, branding or watermark.
