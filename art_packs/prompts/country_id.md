Built-in ImageGen, reference-matched generation mode. The strict Travary screen
and matching Travary shell supplied geometry, tactile paper, shadows and
live-text zones. Palette: Indonesian red and ivory, batik indigo, temple gold,
jungle jade and tropical turquoise.

Balinese split gate, layered rice terraces, volcano, palms, frangipani and a
small outrigger boat toward the right. Include the detachable ivory barcode

watermark.
